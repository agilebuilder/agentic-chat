# Code of Conduct

Agentic Chat follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful, constructive and inclusive. Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, publication of private information and sustained disruption are not acceptable in repository discussions, issues, pull requests or project events.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers listed in [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md). Maintainers will protect reporter privacy, investigate proportionately and may edit, remove, reject or ban participation when necessary.
